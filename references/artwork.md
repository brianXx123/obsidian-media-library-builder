# Artwork provenance / 素材来源

Version 0.3.1 desktop demo has **24/24 real poster or official publicity images**. Sixteen original historical images were retained; eight missing-art titles were replaced with works whose exact artwork has a file-specific public-domain statement. TV works use actual official publicity photographs. Source image files remain local and are excluded from source, Skill and plugin packages. / 桌面演示库24部全部有真实海报或官方宣传图，源文件仅留在隔离本地目录。

**Mobile screenshots were retained byte-for-byte at the user’s request.** They show the separately documented 0.3.0 mobile demo, not the revised desktop list. / 按用户要求，移动截图逐字节保留，桌面与移动演示片单分别记录。

The Jazz Singer source GIF was losslessly decoded to a PNG for the existing raster-image loader; the original source bytes and converted image are retained locally. No AI artwork or invented film poster is used. Image identity, title and artwork date were checked; one unrelated broad-search result for Wings was rejected and replaced by the exact Wings poster file.

Series references: [Bonanza / Paramount](https://paramountglobalcontent.com/title/bonanza), [The Beverly Hillbillies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beverly_Hillbillies). Bonanza season numbers 1–14 are supplied without fabricated episode metadata. Public scores, plots and directors are left unknown. Code MIT licensing does not relicense third-party artwork.
